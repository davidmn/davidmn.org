Xenos by Dan Abnett was a cheeky comfort reread over Christmas. I first read it in the first year of university and it was already old then and now it's 25 years old. Old enough to have opinions on amasec vintages.

Do I recommend it? Hell yes. It's _the_ way to get into 40K fiction, you could probably just read the Eisenhorn series and leave the rest of the 40K extended universe. I've got a couple of other books on the go at the moment to might hold off on starting the sequel for a bit.
