I'm way behind on posting. I've got two book reviews to go up but I've not been motivated to write them. Instead I have spent nearly all my free time think about and playing Music League.

Gav mentioned it on Regular Features [ep 614](https://regularfeatures.com/614-quiet-little-trash-boy/). Beehind Honemy Lines made a league and we were off.

The game is simple. Each round has a theme, you submit songs for that theme, everyone listens to the playlist then votes on their favs. There's a leader board and such but the joy is reading everyone's reviews and finding new music. Also inflicting Leperous on unsuspecting bees.

And I just can't stop. I made a league for the group chat. I made a league at work. I want to play this forever. I love listening to tracks I'd never have found otherwise.
